Slice-a-Rod Packet C1 · Topics 2.1, 5.4

A 1.0 m rod is cut into N slices. Each slice has mass dm = λ(x) dx. Add up the slices to get the mass, the center of mass, and the rotational inertia about the axis you choose. More slices → the sum becomes the integral.
